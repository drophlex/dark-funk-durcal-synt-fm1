BEFORE INSTALLING
1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 by USB (a data cable) with a charged battery.
2. Back up your projects and sounds with the official workbench.
3. Do not unplug or switch off the FM-1 while it writes.
4. To go back to stock: open "Return to official firmware" and pick your FM-1.fwsc (V15 or V16).
